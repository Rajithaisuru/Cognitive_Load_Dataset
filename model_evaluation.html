Project: Cognitive Load Prediction System for Online Learning Using Behavioral Patterns Group ID: R26-SE-004 Researcher: Subasinghe M.H.R.I. - IT22089786 Institution: Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology Date:

You will watch an educational video while anonymous interaction features are used by a fixed cognitive-load model. After each segment, you will give a five-point self-reported mental-effort rating.

  • The model prediction is calculated and saved before your rating, but it remains hidden until your rating is submitted.
  • Your rating is not used as an input to the prediction and does not update or retrain the model.
  • The system stores an anonymous participant/session ID, video and segment IDs, interaction features, prediction, rating, timestamps, and model version/hash.
  • No name, password, or personal identifying information is collected.
