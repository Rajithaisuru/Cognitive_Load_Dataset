Project: Cognitive Load Prediction System for Online Learning Using Behavioral Patterns Group ID: R26-SE-004 Researcher: Subasinghe M.H.R.I. - IT22089786 Institution: Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology Date:

This study collects anonymous learner interaction data while watching educational videos to develop and evaluate a real-time cognitive load prediction system.

If you agree, you will watch a selected educational video clip, interact normally, and provide a short Paas 1-9 mental effort rating after each segment.

  • The system may collect anonymous participant code, session/video ID, video interaction events, time spent on content, and Paas ratings.
  • No name, password, or personal identifying information will be collected.
  • Data will be used only for academic research, model training, evaluation, reporting, and summarized publication.
